ABOUT OUR MISSION

Empowering Gram Sabhas Through Digital Democracy

GramConnect is an initiative designed to bridge the information gap between rural citizens and village local administration.

By digitizing records, grievance logs, and fund allocations, we ensure that every villager has an equal voice in Gram Sabha decisions.
